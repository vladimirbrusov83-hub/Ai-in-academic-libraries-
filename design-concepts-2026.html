Lead Article · The Curriculum

Artificial intelligence, explained for the people who organize knowledge

Seventeen modules across three levels take library professionals from first principles to agentic workflows — written in a practitioner's voice, with honesty about limits.

Sixty-nine percent of academic libraries are now evaluating or implementing artificial intelligence, yet structured training remains scarce. This curriculum answers that gap with three levels — Foundations, Applied, and Advanced — each module readable in fifteen to twenty-five minutes.

Two role-based paths guide the journey: one for practicing librarians in reference, instruction, and research support; another for digital librarians working in systems, metadata, and repositories.
